About Diabetic Retinopathy

Diabetic retinopathy is a complication of diabetes that affects the eyes. It's caused by damage to the blood vessels of the light-sensitive tissue at the back of the eye (retina).

At this early stage (Non-proliferative diabetic retinopathy), the walls of the blood vessels in your retina weaken. Tiny bulges (microaneurysms) protrude from the vessel walls, sometimes leaking fluid and blood into the retina.
